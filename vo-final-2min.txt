Your SaaS isn't stuck because of your product. It's stuck because of your time.

Say you're at ten thousand a month. Everything feels urgent. Support, bugs, sales calls, hiring. You do it all, and growth stays flat. You end every day tired, and the business looks exactly the same as it did last month.

Here's the loop. You're the bottleneck. So work piles up. So you work harder. So you have no time to fix the thing that's causing the pile. Round and round. And the scary part? It feels like progress, because you're always busy.

Break it with three moves.

Move one. Write down every task you did this week. Every single one. Emails, calls, quick fixes, even the five minute favours. Then mark each with how much it's worth per hour.

You'll see a pattern fast. A few tasks make you money. Most just keep the lights on. And those lights-on tasks are quietly eating your best hours.

Move two. Hand off the lights-on tasks. Start with support. Write one short guide, give it to one person, and let them run it. Check in once, then step back. You're not giving up control, you're buying back time.

Don't wait until it's perfect. Good enough, handed off today, beats perfect next quarter.

Move three. Spend the hours you got back on one thing only. The one thing that moves revenue. A better offer. A better onboarding. A better price. Pick one, finish it, then pick the next.

Now run the numbers again. Same week, same you, but twenty hours back. That's twenty hours of sales, or product, or both. Even half of that changes your month.

The loop flips. Free time funds growth. Growth funds help. Help frees more time. Every turn makes the next one easier.

That's the whole game.

Do it this week. Audit on Monday. Hand off on Wednesday. Reinvest on Friday. Then do it again next week.

Stop being the bottleneck. Start being the founder.
